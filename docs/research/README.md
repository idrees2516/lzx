# Research Reports

External-facing research and roadmap documents for the LZX stack.

- `LZX_Next_Generation_zkVM_Research_Report.pdf` (2026-10): the Wave-8
  research report - the SOTA zkVM landscape (SP1, Jolt, RISC Zero, Valida,
  OpenVM, Pico, Nexus, ZisK/LambdaClass), the prioritized next-papers
  pipeline (prover/verifier time, 50-100 KB proofs, privacy, distributed
  proving, IVC/PCD/folding, soundness and security), the 100 M cycles/sec
  engineering budget, the GPU acceleration plan (cudarc + tensor-core MAC/
  NTT kernels), the Bend/HVM + Lean formal-verification tracks, and the
  immediate SALSAA + Twist-and-Shout completion plan (chapter 11).
